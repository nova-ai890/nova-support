Nova AI Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nova AI handles information when you use the Nova AI application and related services.

Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, Nova AI may process information such as account and sign-in information, prompts and conversations, files or images you choose to upload, app usage information, subscription or purchase status, and information you provide when contacting support.

How We Use Information

We may use information to provide and operate Nova AI, generate responses and other requested content, maintain accounts and preferences, provide app features, manage usage limits and subscriptions, improve reliability and safety, prevent abuse, troubleshoot problems, and provide customer support.

AI Features and Service Providers

When you use AI-powered features, information needed to fulfill your request may be processed by service providers that help operate those features. We may also use service providers for infrastructure, authentication, subscriptions, analytics, safety, and other functions necessary to operate Nova AI. Their handling of information is subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

Files, Images, Voice, and Other Content

If you choose to submit files, images, audio, voice input, or other content, that information may be processed to provide the feature you requested. Please avoid submitting sensitive information that is not necessary for your request.

Purchases

Purchases made through Apple are processed by Apple. Nova AI may receive information about your subscription or entitlement status needed to provide paid features, but does not receive your full payment-card details from Apple.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate Nova AI, comply with applicable law, protect users and the service, or complete actions you request.

Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce applicable agreements. Retention may vary depending on the type of information and feature involved.

Your Choices

You may choose not to provide optional information or use features that require certain information. For privacy questions or requests concerning your information, contact us using the email below. We may need to verify a request before completing it.

Children's Privacy

Nova AI is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, please contact us.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect information. However, no electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Nova AI evolves. The effective date at the top of this page will indicate when the policy was last updated.
